Step 1: Download your guide

This is a Word file. Download it to your computer. You do not need a Google account.

Download the fillable guide (Word file)

Step 2: Complete it

  1. Open the file in Word.
  2. Read each step. The content comes from SBA.gov, the U.S. government's small business website.
  3. Type your answers in the empty boxes. The boxes grow as you type.
  4. Type your name, class, team business name, and date at the top of page 1.

Step 3: Save as a PDF and turn it in

  1. Click File, then Save As (or Print), and choose PDF.
  2. Name the file with your last name and class period.
  3. Submit the PDF for your grade. Your finished PDF is your assessment.
