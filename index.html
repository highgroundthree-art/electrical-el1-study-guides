Guides

One plain-English study guide for each module your student studies. Read it together, talk it over, and help your student explain what they are learning.

Draft for instructor review

These guides are companions to the NCCER textbook, not copies of it. They are for learning and talking, and they are not permission to do electrical work. No student names or grades appear here, and nothing you tick or tap on these pages is saved or sent anywhere.

How to use a guide

Plan about 15 to 25 minutes per section. Your student does most of the talking, and you do not need to know the answers ahead of time. If your student can read it and explain it, they are learning it.
